Privacy Policy — Match & Learn

Developer: Whiteheart Inc. · Last updated: November 2026
Child-Safe Summary: Match & Learn is designed for children and families. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information from children or adults. All game progress and audio assets work locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Match & Learn does not require account registration, login, email addresses, names, location data, camera, microphone, or contacts access. We do not collect analytics tied to individual users or children.

2. Local Device Storage

Game progress (completed levels, stars, discovered fun facts, selected language, and audio preferences) is stored solely in local storage on your device and never transmitted to our servers. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

3. Offline Audio & Educational Content

All illustrations, educational facts, sound effects, and voice narrations are bundled directly inside the app for safe, uninterrupted offline play.

4. Parental Gate & In-App Purchases

Optional one-time purchases (such as unlocking all categories or removing banners) are protected behind a math-based Parental Gate and processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. Whiteheart Inc. never receives or stores payment card information.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us.
